Put the MeshLineExtrudeEditor in the editor folder. 
How to use this:
Preparation: Create a 3D road segment (a simple box or a complex mesh). Ensure it is oriented so its "forward" axis is Z.
Setup: Create an Empty GameObject, add MeshLineExtruder, and drag your road mesh into the Source Mesh slot.
Drawing:
Select the GameObject.
In the Scene View, hold Shift and Left Click on your terrain or floor.
The mesh will automatically "shrinkwrap" (snap) to the surface where you click and extrude a segment from the previous point.
Lateral Leveling: Because of the Quaternion.LookRotation(direction, Vector3.up) logic, the road will stay flat horizontally even if you are clicking up a steep mountain side. 

If you find yourself accidentally moving the terrain while drawing:
Go to the Layers dropdown (top right of Unity).
Click the Lock icon next to your "Terrain" layer.
Now you can click the terrain to get the height data, but you won't accidentally select or move the terrain object itself!
